# Expedition

As the GM it is your role to create the expedition. The expedition includes the
ecosystems and how they are connected, the denizens of those ecosystems, and the
Ghastly Mutated that puts them all under threat.

The mutated will set out on the expedition to defeat the Ghastly Mutated. They
will explore the ecosystems, gathering Body Parts and tracking down the Ghastly
Mutated before it causes irreparable damage.

## Creating Expeditions

### 1. Connected Ecosystems

The first step to creating an expedition is to select your ecosystems and
connect them. This will be the wilderness that the Mutated can explore. The
Wilderness is mapped as a point crawl where each ecosystem is connected to one
or more other ecosystems. Here you will want to expand on the descriptions of
the ecosystems to include how the mutated travel from this ecosystem to
connected ones. For example are there cave openings or gates, or rivers that
take them from this ecosystem to the other.

> [!NOTE]
> If want to decide a weather hex in advance you can do this now as well.
