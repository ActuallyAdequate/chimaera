# Hazards

Your role as GM is to introduce adversity to the Mutated so they can fight,
problem solve, and adventure. You do not require Hazards to create these
situations for the Mutated, this is something left to you to control the pacing
and difficulty.

Hazards are a way for additional problems to occur in response to the Mutated's
Action. The more the Mutated push their luck by failing Challenges, the more
hostile their Environment becomes.

Below are is a list of how Hazards can be spent when they are given to You.

- **Immediate Consequence:** You immediately spend the Hazard to create a
  drawback to the Action.
  > The Mutated fails the Challenge to make an Attack, you immediately spend the
  > Hazard to reduce the Injuries dealt to 0
  >
  > The Mutated fails sneaking past a Guard, Unfortunately they step on a stick
  > that makes a loud cracking noise
  >
  > The Mutated lies about who they know at a party, the Creature is familiar
  > with this creature and calls the Mutated's bluff
  >
  > The Mutated ambushes some creatures but fails the challenge, they still
  > manage to Attack them by surprise, but one Creature has just enough time to
  > scream for reinforcements.

- **Ecosystem Hostility:** Allocate it to one of the hostility changes for the
  ecosystem that the Mutated are in.
  > You spend two of your saved Hazards to unlock the Sulphur Leaks in the Dark
  > Caves

- **Weather Change:** Move the Marker for the Weather in a direction of your
  choice.
  > You spend the 3 Hazards to move the Weather Marker 3 places up. The Weather
  > is now Scorching resulting the Exhausted Condition.

- **Ambushes and Traps:** Save The Hazard to create an event while the Mutated
  are travelling, camping or exploring.
  > The Mutated are travelling from the Quiet Woods to the Flooded River, where
  > they hear rumbling in the ground. Tunneling vultures crash out of the ground
  > taking the Mutated by surprise.
  >
  > The Mutated set up camp and sleep through the night, a sneaky snake creature
  > visits them in the night and poisons their Flooded
  >
  > The Mutated are exploring the ecosystem, as they are breaking through to a
  > clearing the snap of a bear trap entraps the leaders leg.

- **Passing Challenges:** Make Creature automatically pass a Challenge.
  > A Creature attacks the Mutated but fails the Challenge, By spending a hazard
  > they exert more effort into attacking and thus deal Injuries.
  >
  > A Creature attempts to sniff out the Mutated from their hiding place, By
  > spending a Hazard they catch the scent of the Mutated.
