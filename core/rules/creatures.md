# The Creatures of Chimaera

Chimaera is host to a wide variety of creatures. Peaceful herds wander wastes.
Predator packs stalk prey with gnashing jaws. In the darkness parasitic
aberrations slink into crevasses. Intelligent denizens hide in fear from winged
monstrosities. Creatures form a critical component of the ecosystems the Mutated
explore. While some creatures are aggressive others may be tricky, some may be
friendly, and some may be deadly. It is up to the Mutated to determine how they
will interact, avoid, or defeat the Creatures of Chimaera

## Type of Creatures

### Beasts

Beasts are at the bottom of the hierarchy of Mutanism. When these creatures were
exposed to the Serum. They underwent grotesque mutations and fusions. They are
the most common of creatures, often, but not always, unintelligent. Unlike other
creatures they did not gain the ability to Mutate at will.

### Mutanous

Mutanous Creatures are also common place in Chimaera. The Serum unlocked their
genome allowing them to mutated with other creatures and body parts to change
their body into different forms. Mutanous creatures are extremely adaptable to
their ecosystem, and are able to change niches withing their ecosystem. They are
often not capable of language, but that does not mean they don't have
complicated communication.

### Denizens

Creatures that mutated with or from the Precursors DNA were able to capture
their intelligence ingenuity. These creatures form the few societies of
Chimaera, they are capable of complex language, social interactions, and have
tendencies of exploration. The Mutated are among the denizens of Chimaera.

### The Ghastly Mutated

This creature is of a chaotic and threatening nature. Its reach is far across
many ecosystems, and it has the strength, influence or cunning to dramatically
alter the ecosystems that the creatures of Chimaera live in. The infection of
the Serum elevated the Ghastly Mutated by unpredictable degrees turning them
into ravenous and ferocious creatures.

## Hunting the Ghastly Mutated

The goal of your expedition is to defeat the Ghastly Mutated. These creatures
are extraordinary, threatening and difficult to defeat. Your expedition starts
when you witness the Ghastly Mutated's devastation, hear rumours of its
presence, or are ambushed by it directly. Finding the Ghastly Mutated's
Ecosystem is the first piece of the puzzle. Below are somethings you may do to
Track the Ghastly Mutated:

- Follow sightings of the Ghastly Mutated
- Track other Creatures that are drones of the Ghastly Mutated
- Ask Denizens that live in different ecosystems
- Trace tracks or destruction through the wilderness

In addition to finding the Ghastly Mutated habitat you will need to prepare to
defeat to it. This involves identifying how the Ghastly Mutated is connected to
it ecosystem, what Body Parts will be effective in defeating it. Will the
weather be extreme. Is it alone or does it have other creatures that will defend
it. Preparing can be done in some of these ways:

- Visiting Societies for knowledge and resources
- Exploring ecosystems and helping or fighting its creatures
- Delving into Facilities for Serum, Body Parts or its contraptions
- Observing the Ghastly Mutated
- Scouting out the Ghastly Mutated's Habitat

The final step of the hunt and the expedition's conclusion happens when you
defeat the Ghastly Mutated

- You may fight it in its habitat
- You may trap it or scare it off
- You may lure it out to deal with it on your own grounds
