# Environment

Not only are the creatures of Chimaera dangerous and horrid but so to is the
environment. The environment of Chimaera is chaotic, the weather inhospitable,
the terrain impenetrable.

## Ecosystem

Each encounter takes place in an ecosystem, the ecosystem refers to the flora
and fauna present and how they interact. Sometimes an Ecosystem is peaceful,
sometime alive and vibrant, other times nothing but hostile. Ecosystems are
connected to each other, the Mutated will go on expeditions to explore the
Ecosystem, meet Colonies, and discover Facilities The GM can spend Hazards to
increase the Ecosystem's Hostility, causing it to evolve. See the GM section for
examples of Ecosystems.

## Weather

Each encounter may also be subject to extreme weather. Weather often grants
conditions that effect what creatures are able to do. The weather changes
randomly when travelling from one ecosystem to another, however, the GM is able
to spend Hazards to move the weather to greater extremes. See the GM section for
Weather.

## Societies

The denizens of Chimaera will often form societies. Cooperation and Symbiotic
living are usually the only way to keep safe in the turmoil of the wilderness.
The structures and intricacies of societies vary by its members. In broad stokes
here are some of the societies that the Mutated may come across.

### Herds and Packs and Schools

These are creatures that have formed nomadic groups, migrating long distances
across Chimaera's ecosystems. Some walk periodically, some never-endingly. These
creatures are often united by purpose whether it is the hunt, the journey or
protection.

### Colonies

When societies get large they grow into colonies. These are the most organized
of societies hosting hundreds or thousands of denizens. Colonies may be run by
inner communities of intelligent leaders, dominated by malicious apex predators,
or bound by queens of hive minds. Colonies usually begin to approach some of the
sophistication of the Precursor cities, creatures may build complex underground
chambers, construct mounds and nests, or dams and bridges.

Colonies usually provide respite for the Mutated to rest and repair. You may
form relationships, alliances or rivalries with the denizens. Your home may be a
colony that you need to defend from the Ghastly Mutated.

## Facilities

The remnants of the Precursors can be found in the Facilities. This structures
are beyond comprehension from a species that had mastered nature. These
structures house machination dangerous and incomprehensible. Some structures
hows horrid creatures even more mutanous than whats in the wilderness. Other
Facilities alter their ecosystems or the weather, and some hold reservoirs of
serum that can be used to repair Body Parts.

Exploring the Facilities will equip The Mutated with what they need to defeat
the Ghastly Mutated
