# Precursor Artifacts

Artifacts of the Precursors can be found in the world of chimaera. The knowledge
of how to make these items has long been forgotten. This makes them rare and
valuable to find in the wilderness, but many of these artifacts can be
discovered in the facilities. Precursor Artifacts are used to operate the
contraptions found in Facilities or the wider Wilderness. They can be used to
power contraptions, steer, control or stop them.

## Contraptions

A contraption refers to any machinery built by the Precursors. These include the
large systems found in Facilities, breaker boxes that power buildings and
machines. Contraptions include turnstile doors or escalators, conveyor belts,
doors, security systems, science equipment, traps, anything that is a permanent
structure built and once operated by the Precursors.

## Artifacts

Below is a list of contraptions. They are categorised by type. Some Body Parts
reduce the Challenge of operating contraptions when using a particular type of
Artifact.

### Power

- **Batteries:** Used to Power Contraptions for a limited amount of time.
- **Wire:** Used to connect power between contraptions
- **Generator:** Used to power contraption for an extended amount of time while
  fueled (does not need wire to connect to contraption)
- **Fuse:** Used to repair Breaker Boxes to power a Facility

> [!INFO] Power Artifacts do not work while submerged in liquid

### Mechanical

- **Crank Shaft:** Used to rotate contraptions
- **Hydraulic Jack:** Used to raise contraptions
- **Rope:** Used to tie contraptions together can also be used to restrain
  creatures
- **Trap:** A Trap can be setup and triggered when a Creature goes near it. Trap
  can be set up in the following ways:
  - Lethal Trap: Deal 2 Physical Injuries when triggered
  - Detection Trap: Makes a loud noise when triggered
  - Contraption Trap: Activate a contraption when triggered

### Keys and Controllers

- **Mag Card:** Used to open magnetically sealed doors.
- **Metal Key:** Used to open locked doors
- **Remote Controller:** Pair to a contraption to control it remotely, the
  remote controller can only be paired to one contraption at a time. The
  distance of control is unlimited
- **Clock:** Allows contraptions to be operated on a timer

### Containers

- **Jar:** Used to hold a liquid or powder or gas.
- **Plastic Bag:** Allows a creature to carry two Artifacts in the Bag
- **Water Proof Case:** Allows Power Artifacts to function while submerged

### Chemicals

- **Glue:** used to bind contraptions together
- **Fuel:** Used to Power the Generator Artifact, Fuel can also be ignited to
  Grant the Burning Condition to anything Touching items
- **Serum:** used to Repair a Body Part to Healthy.
- **Ignition Reagent:** used to ignite a flammable contraption.

### Carrying Artifacts

To carry an artifact the Mutated must place the Artifact on one of their Limbs.
This Limb can not be used while the Artifact is being carried. It is encumbered
until the Artifact is dropped.

### Using Artifacts

Precursor Artifacts are complicated and the knowledge of how to use them as long
gone. When Using an Artifact or Operating a contraption with an Artifact you
must execute a Challenge. The following Artifacts do not require a Challenge to
use.

- Mag Card or Metal Keys used to unlock doors do not require a Challenge. But do
  when unlocking other Contraptions per the GMs discretion.
- Container Artifacts do not require a Challenge to store items.
- Serum does not require a Challenge to Repair Body Parts
