# Chimaera

Chimaera is a hack and slash role-playing game set in a world where one must
mutate their body with all manner of crude biological weaponry. Tack on extra
limbs and become the arachnid monstrosity. Alter your brain and become a
psychological freak. Will you tear through your enemies claiming their body
parts into a mound of trophies worn on your back, or will you carefully select
what body parts are sanitary to become a keen and precise instrument of death?

## Playing Chimaera

In order to play, one will need a group, most of which will be willing to
undergo medical procedures without a qualified surgeon, and a complete disregard
for all sanitation in any situation. One member of the group will be the Ghastly
Mutated or GM. The GM will be responsible for running the game and ensuring the
group is tried and tested before facing the horrid creature of the same name.

This book will outline rules for the players, referred to as the Mutated, this
will provide information for creating a creature, the key rules of the game, and
a menagerie of specimens detailing various Body Parts they can look forward to
carelessly binding to themselves. Following this will be more about the world of
Chimaera, horrors, mysteries, wildness and all. To close are the notes for the
GM, this will contain suggestions for how to run the game, tips on how to design
a session, as well as a heaving mound of gory tools to aid in the whole process.

## The Expedition

You are the Mutated, a team of creatures trying to survive the wild and chaotic
environment of Chimaera. Beasts, conspiracies, storms, and wilderness are among
the normal challenges of life in Chimaera. However, you have been alerted to a
new threat, one that risks the entire habitat to collapse. Those that have
encountered it fear its devastation. It is known as the Ghastly Mutated. In its
wake is a destruction unlike any other, but also an opportunity. In an
unforgiving world where the weak die, defeating the Ghastly Mutated will prove
to every denizen that you, the Mutated, are capable denizens.

The hunt for the Ghastly Mutated is a long one. You will be collecting Body
Parts, throwing away broken ones and gaining all sorts of defects and issues.
You will engage in brawls, espionage, politics, crawl through caves to fight
evil slugs, or brave frigid mountains plagued with blizzards. Finally, you will
attempt to bring an end to the Ghastly Mutated, either defeating it in glory or
failing and cowering away licking your wounds.
