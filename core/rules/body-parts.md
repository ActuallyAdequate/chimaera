# Body Parts

Each Body Part has four elements which describe its behaviour.

1. **Body System:** The Body System it belongs to.
2. **Disrepair:** How Many Injuries the Body Part has taken.
3. **Description:** A description of the Body Part that can be used to help
   describe how it is used in an Action.
4. **Abilities:** Descriptions of how the body part interacts with rules such as
   Movement, Reach, Injuries and Conditions.

> [!QUESTION] Do I have to use all abilities on the Body Part when Activated?
> No, you can choose any of the relevant abilities to execute when Activating a
> Body Part. In some cases your justification for your action may not allow you
> to execute every Ability, as they may contradict the intended goal.

## Key Terms

### Movement

Movement describes the distance a creature can traverse land, air, or sea. There
a different types of movement.

- **Run:** Movement across land
- **Jump:** Movement horizontally and vertically through the air, but beginning
  and ending on land
- **Climb:** Movement on walls and ceilings
- **Swim:** Movement on or in liquid
- **Dig:** Movement through soft material
- **Roll:** Unpowered Movement on flat or declined land
- **Fly:** Movement horizontally and vertically through air
- **Glide:** Unpowered Movement horizontally and downward through air
- **Drop:** Movement downward through air or liquid
- **Squeeze:** Movement through tight spaces about the size of a keyhole

> [!INFO] Challenging Movement Sometime Movement is Challenging, this means that
> in order to move the Creature must execute a Challenge. Some Examples of
> Challenging Movement:
>
> - Running in Mud
> - Swim in a current
> - Digging in coarse material

### Range

Range specifies the distance that a Body Part's Ability effects. Such as being
able to Throw something to a distance of Near. Or Spikes being able to Reach a
distance of Personal Space. This means that the target must be within that
distance to be affected.

Attaching a Body Part to another Body Part inherits that Range of the Body Part
it is attached to, unless the attached Body Part also specifies its own Range.
For example Attaching a Body Part that doesn't specify its own Range will have
the default range of the Body of Personal Space. But if it specifies its own
Range such as Near then it has a Range of Near.

There a different types of Range

- **Reach:** Represents melee range
- **Throw** Represents the range of something thrown
- **Shove** Represents the range that you can move another creature

### Senses

Senses allow creatures to observe there surroundings and detect hazards, other
creatures, and find hidden details. By default Creatures have a basic set of
senses, they can see in good lighting, they can smell pungent odours. Body Parts
provide stronger or additional senses, some examples of additional senses are
echolocation which allows the creature to pin point the location of creatures
and objects, movement detection which allows the creature to detect movement
around them without sight, or a heightened sense of smell or taste, which allow
the detection of creatures by smell, or a breakdown of ingredients in a
concoction.

There a different types of Senses:

- **See:** Ability to observe visually
- **Smell:** Ability to observe odours
- **Hear:** Ability to observe sound
- **Touch:** Ability to observe texture
- **Taste:** Ability to observe flavour
- **Echolocate:** Ability to locate entities with sound
- **Detect Movement:** Ability to locate moving entities with tremors

### Communication

Many Creatures including the Mutated are capable of verbal communication. This
allows the Mutated to scheme, alert, chat, and sing. Two Creatures can
communicate provided at least one Creature has the Ability to execute the method
of communication, and the Receiving Creature has not inhibited the Body System
to receive the Communication. For example any creature can understand sign
language provided they are able to sense it (See or Detect Movement). But a
creature can only convey information with sign language if they have a Body Part
that allows them to Communicate with Sign Language.

Common Types of Communication:

- **Voice:** Verbal Communication to those that have an uninhibited Auditory
  System
- **Sign Language:** Communication through motion to those that have an
  uninhibited Sight System or with the sense Detect Motion
- **Colour:** Communication through patterns and colours to those that have an
  uninhibited Sight System
- **Pheromones:** Communication through odour to those that have an uninhibited
  Aromatic System
