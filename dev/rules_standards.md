# Rules Standards
This defines the wording and style guide for rules.

## Passive 

(When) [Condition] [Action] for [Restriction]

Restrictions: This limb, All Body Parts, This Creature

## Movement

[**Mode**] (Restriction) to [Distance]

- Modes: Run Jump Climb Swim Dig Fly
- Distance: Close Near Distant Afar
- Restriction: Vertically Horizontally Walls Ceilings

## Range

[**Mode**] to [Distance]

- Modes: Reach Throw
- Distance: Personal Space Close Near Distant Afar

## Injury

[**Deal**|**Ignore**] [Value] [Type] Injury (to the [Body System] system) (to this Limb)

- Value: 1-3
- Type: Physical Internal
- Body System: Muscular Integumentary Aromatic Visual Auditory Circulatory Thought Endocrine

## Conditions 

([Scope] Ignores | Grant) the [Condition] condition (for|from|excluding [Restriction])

- Scope: This Body, This Limb, Body System
- Condition: Freezing Boiling Acid Poison
- Restriction:Something ingested,creatures in your pack, creatures that see you, creatures that smell you.
